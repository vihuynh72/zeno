Short Paper 2: Design and Attention

Due Friday, November 6, 2026, by 11:59 PM on Canvas

Prompt

Many apps are built to keep us scrolling. Pick one app you use every week. Is its design a form of manipulation, or a fair way to compete for our attention? Take a side, give your two best reasons, and answer the strongest objection to your view.

Length

900 to 1,200 words (about 3 to 4 pages, double-spaced).

Requirements

  1. State your thesis in the first paragraph.
  2. Cite at least two course readings, with page numbers.
