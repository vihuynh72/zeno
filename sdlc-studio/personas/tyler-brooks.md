# Tyler Brooks

> **Status:** Draft, awaiting Vi's approval. The Negative persona: Zeno is not built for him.

## Quick Reference

| Attribute | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Cast role** | negative |
| **Role** | College sophomore |
| **Context** | Wants AI to produce finished work and submit it for him |

## Who They Are

Tyler is 19. He already uses AI chat to write his discussion posts and essays, and he wants a tool that does all of
it, end to end, including the submitting.

## End Goals (stated to exclude)

1. Have AI write his essays and answers.
2. Have assignments submitted for him.
3. Spend as little of his own time on coursework as possible.

## Why we are not designing for them

Zeno's mission is the opposite: "Other AI does your work. Zeno gets you to do it." The trust rules say Zeno never does
the work wholesale and never submits anything. Building for Tyler would turn Zeno into the "general chat wrapper" Vi
ruled out, and it would put students' academic integrity at risk.

## Behaviours & Context

- **Environment:** a laptop, usually an hour before something is due.
- **Frequency:** only when a deadline is close.
- **Proficiency:** skilled at prompting AI chat; avoids planning of any kind.

## Frustrations

- Assignments that need his own thinking.
- AI answers that a teacher can spot.
- Tools that "make him do it".

## How to handle a request from them

"Write my essay" gets one honest line and the first step: "I won't write it. Let's find your first claim: 10
minutes." (Live: Zeno refuses and offers to plan it.) Subject help, when it ships, may suggest an outline or a first
sentence, but he writes the work.
