## Who They Are

Daniel is 34 and leads a launch due early next year. His days are back-to-back meetings, so the real work (specs,
decisions, reviews) waits until 7 PM. AI drafts his documents, but he still starts every morning in Slack and ends
every evening in his inbox. He used to run three times a week. This month he ran once.

## End Goals

*Most important first.*

1. Know the one thing that matters most right now, across all his tools.
2. Protect time for deep work on his big project, not just meetings and messages.
3. Keep his promises to people (reviews, replies, follow-ups) without living in his inbox.
4. Keep a life goal alive in busy weeks: run three times a week.

## Experience Goals

- In control of his week, not chased by it.
- Treated like an adult: straight talk, no cheerleading.
- Fast: everything is one tap or one sentence.
- Not another inbox.

## Behaviours & Context

- **Environment:** a laptop at his desk and in meeting rooms; his phone in the gaps between meetings.
- **Frequency:** a look first thing in the morning, then in the 10-20 minute gaps between meetings.
- **Proficiency:** a power user of every work tool. He hates setup and will not maintain another system.

## Frustrations

- Six tools hold six lists, and none of them knows what matters most.
- Every tool notifies, so he muted them all, and important things slip.
- Jira and to-do apps need upkeep he never has time for.
- People wait on him for a review or a reply, and he forgets until they ping him.
- Deep work loses to meetings, and his runs are the first thing to go.

## Scenario: a day with Zeno

**Monday, 8:30 AM.** Daniel's card reads "Write the launch risk list · 20 min · Q1 launch · review Thu", and Zeno
says why in one line: "First: Priya is waiting on it for Thursday." Between meetings he works in 20-minute steps. On
his iPhone the stopwatch sits on the lock screen, and he taps Done there when a step is finished.

**12:10 PM.** He tells Zeno, "Move my run to Thursday morning." It shows up on his main calendar as "[Moved] Run", in
Zeno's color, with no reminder. A Slack thread where his manager asks for a decision lands in the launch project
without him copying anything.

**Sunday evening.** "2 goals moving, 1 slipping: you ran once. Plan the week?" One tap, and three runs land on his
calendar as commitments around his meetings.
