## Who They Are

Rosa is 39. She left college at 20 and went back last year to finish a bachelor's in business administration online,
two courses a term, aiming for spring 2028. She works 8 to 4:30 and picks up her kids (9 and 12) at 5; Saturdays are
soccer. Her free time comes in small windows: a half-hour lunch, and after 9 PM when the house is quiet. She also
wants to walk three times a week.

## End Goals

*Most important first.*

1. Finish her degree by spring 2028 without falling behind a term.
2. Use the small windows she has on the right thing.
3. Keep her family commitments and her own habit (walking three times a week) without guilt.
4. Never set up or keep up a planner: tell it once, and it keeps track.

## Experience Goals

- Quiet: Zeno fits around her life and never asks for more of it.
- Forgiven: a missed night is simply moved, not counted against her.
- Proud: she can see the degree getting closer.

## Behaviours & Context

- **Environment:** mostly her phone; the laptop for exams and long papers.
- **Frequency:** two or three short visits a day, usually lunch and late evening.
- **Proficiency:** comfortable with email and Canvas; no patience for productivity systems.

## Frustrations

- Every planner she tried needed a Sunday planning session she never had time for.
- When a week slides, she cannot tell whether 2028 is still on track, so she worries.
- Reminders arrive at dinner or during pickup, never when she can act on them.
- Study apps assume she is 18 with free afternoons.

## Scenario: a week with Zeno

**Start of the term.** In Plan mode she drops her two syllabi onto her "Degree" goal. Zeno lays out the term as
weekly projects and steps. She does not open Plan mode again until next term.

**Tuesday, 9:10 PM, on her phone.** The card reads "Read section 4.2 · 15 min · ACCT 201 quiz · due Sun". She taps
Start, then Done: "Nice. Next: 5 practice problems, 15 min." She taps "That's enough today." Zeno saves her place.

**Wednesday.** She misses her walk, a Commitment. Zeno quietly moves it to Thursday at lunch. Reorganizing her notes,
a Nice-to-have, slides without a word.

**Friday.** Section 4.3 has slid twice. The next time she opens Zeno, one line waits on the card: "Section 4.3 slid
twice. [Tomorrow] [Let it go]." She taps Tomorrow. No notification ever came.

**Sunday, 8 PM.** "Degree moving, walks slipping. Plan the week?" One tap, and the week fits around pickups and
soccer.

**What she needs beyond Maya:** goals and projects over months (C2-C4), commitments and missed-work triage (E1), the
weekly check-up (C6), and an app on her phone's home screen (K2). All of it is Later; today she gets the card, focus
and Done.

## Why she is in the cast

Vi asked how people can manage their projects, their life and their really long-term goals with something that
doesn't make them change their life: "just be a quiet part in their life". Maya's semester runs on deadlines that
Canvas sets for her. Daniel's work runs on meetings and other people's asks. Rosa's degree has no one setting the pace
for two years, and her life leaves her the least spare attention of the three. If Zeno manages Rosa's long-term goal
quietly, it can manage anyone's. A grad student with a thesis was the other candidate; Maya and Rosa together cover
that case.
